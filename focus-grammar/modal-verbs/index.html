What are Modal Verbs?
Little helper words! 💬
Rules
must · mustn't
Advice
should · shouldn't
Ability
can · can't
Possibility
might
Careful — these are different!
mustn't 🚫
It is NOT allowed. You mustn't park here. No parking — it's the rule!
don't have to 😌
It is not necessary. You don't have to park here. You can park somewhere else if you want.
