💡 Pet care advice 💡

The law protects our pets. It says we must look after them well and we must give them the things they need to be healthy and happy. Do you do these things for your pet?

  1. Environment
    Do you give your pet a safe and comfortable place to live?
  2. Diet
    Do you give your pet the right food and the right amount of food?
  3. Behaviour
    Do you allow your pet to behave normally?
  4. Company
    Do you give your pet the company it needs (animal or human)?
  5. Health
    Do you protect your pet from injuries and pain?
