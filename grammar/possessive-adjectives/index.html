MY
I → my  (it belongs to me!)
This is my book.
my + noun (book) ✓
I love my backpack.
my + noun (backpack) ✓
She is my friend.
my + noun (friend) ✓
YOUR
you → your  (it belongs to you!)
Where is your ruler?
your + noun (ruler) ✓
I like your lunch box.
your + noun (lunch box) ✓
Is that your sister?
your + noun (sister) ✓
HIS
he → his  (it belongs to him!)
That is his soccer ball.
his + noun (soccer ball) ✓
I see his jacket.
his + noun (jacket) ✓
Tom is his best friend.
his + noun (best friend) ✓
HER
she → her  (it belongs to her!)
I like her doll.
her + noun (doll) ✓
This is her notebook.
her + noun (notebook) ✓
Where is her umbrella?
her + noun (umbrella) ✓
ITS
it → its  (for animals & things!)
The cat wags its tail.
its + noun (tail) ✓
The dog eats its food.
its + noun (food) ✓
The school has its own garden.
its + noun (garden) ✓
OUR
we → our  (it belongs to us!)
This is our classroom.
our + noun (classroom) ✓
We love our garden.
our + noun (garden) ✓
That is our dog.
our + noun (dog) ✓
THEIR
they → their  (it belongs to them!)
I see their house.
their + noun (house) ✓
I like their car.
their + noun (car) ✓
This is their teacher.
their + noun (teacher) ✓
