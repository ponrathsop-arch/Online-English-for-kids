Irregular Verbs
Form changes!
⚠️ Verb 3 is often different from Verb 2!
go → went → gone  ·  eat → ate → eaten
be → been
to exist or describe a state  ·  was/were → been
Verb 1PresentI am happy today.
Verb 2PastI was happy yesterday.
Verb 3PerfectI have been happy all week.
go→gone
to move to a place  ·  went → gone (not "went"!)
Verb 1PresentShe goes to the park every day.
Verb 2PastShe went to the park yesterday.
Verb 3PerfectShe has gone to the park already.
eat→eaten
to put food in your mouth  ·  ate → eaten (not "ate"!)
Verb 1PresentI eat breakfast every morning.
Verb 2PastI ate breakfast an hour ago.
Verb 3PerfectI have eaten breakfast already.
have→had
to own or experience something  ·  had → had (same as Verb 2)
Verb 1PresentI have a bike.
Verb 2PastI had a bike last year.
Verb 3PerfectI have had this bike for two years.
see→seen
to notice with your eyes  ·  saw → seen (not "saw"!)
Verb 1PresentI see my friend every day.
Verb 2PastI saw my friend yesterday.
Verb 3PerfectI have seen my friend today.
do→done
to perform an action or task  ·  did → done (not "did"!)
Verb 1PresentI do my homework every day.
Verb 2PastI did my homework last night.
Verb 3PerfectI have done my homework already.
come→come
to move toward a place or person  ·  came → come (same as base word!)
Verb 1PresentShe comes to class on time.
Verb 2PastShe came to class early yesterday.
Verb 3PerfectShe has come to class already.
get→got
to receive or obtain something  ·  got → got (US: gotten)
Verb 1PresentI get a letter every week.
Verb 2PastI got a letter yesterday.
Verb 3PerfectI have got a new letter today.
give→given
to hand something to someone  ·  gave → given (not "gave"!)
Verb 1PresentI give gifts on birthdays.
Verb 2PastI gave her a gift yesterday.
Verb 3PerfectI have given her a gift already.
take→taken
to carry or move something with you  ·  took → taken (not "took"!)
Verb 1PresentI take photos on every trip.
Verb 2PastI took a photo yesterday.
Verb 3PerfectI have taken many photos today.
make→made
to create or produce something  ·  made → made (same as Verb 2)
Verb 1PresentI make my bed every morning.
Verb 2PastI made my bed earlier.
Verb 3PerfectI have made my bed already.
know→known
to have information or understanding  ·  knew → known (not "knew"!)
Verb 1PresentI know the answer.
Verb 2PastI knew the answer yesterday.
Verb 3PerfectI have known her for years.
think→thought
to use your mind to form ideas  ·  thought → thought (same as Verb 2)
Verb 1PresentI think about it every day.
Verb 2PastI thought about it last night.
Verb 3PerfectI have thought about it a lot.
buy→bought
to get something by paying money  ·  bought → bought (same as Verb 2)
Verb 1PresentI buy milk every week.
Verb 2PastI bought milk yesterday.
Verb 3PerfectI have bought milk already.

Verb 1PresentI write a letter every month.

🧠 Quick Patterns!
the formula
have / has + Verb 3  ·  he/she/it → has  ·  I/you/we/they → have
-en / -n
eat→eaten  ·  see→seen  ·  give→given  ·  take→taken  ·  write→written  ·  fly→flown
-ought / -aught
buy→bought  ·  think→thought  ·  teach→taught  ·  bring→brought
i → u
drink→drunk  ·  sing→sung  ·  swim→swum  ·  ring→rung
no change!
come→come  ·  run→run  ·  put→put  ·  cut→cut
⚠️ V2 ≠ V3!
go: went→gone  ·  eat: ate→eaten  ·  see: saw→seen  ·  do: did→done
