Possessive Pronouns

mine

MINE
my → mine  (I own it!)
This is my book.
→ The book is mine.
This is my pencil.
→ The pencil is mine.
This is my backpack.
→ The backpack is mine.
YOURS
your → yours  (you own it!)
This is your ruler.
→ The ruler is yours.
This is your hat.
→ The hat is yours.
This is your lunch box.
→ The lunch box is yours.
HIS
his → his  (he owns it!)
This is his bike.
→ The bike is his.
This is his soccer ball.
→ The soccer ball is his.
This is his jacket.
→ The jacket is his.
HERS
her → hers  (she owns it!)
This is her doll.
→ The doll is hers.
This is her notebook.
→ The notebook is hers.
This is her umbrella.
→ The umbrella is hers.
ITS
We use its for animals & things!
The cat is in its basket.
The bird is eating its food.
The rabbit is in its cage.
OURS
our → ours  (we own it!)
This is our classroom.
→ The classroom is ours.
This is our garden.
→ The garden is ours.
This is our toy box.
→ The toy box is ours.
THEIRS
their → theirs  (they own it!)
This is their car.
→ The car is theirs.
This is their house.
→ The house is theirs.
This is their dog.
→ The dog is theirs.
